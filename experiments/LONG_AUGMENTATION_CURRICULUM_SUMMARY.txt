100-epoch aggressive augmentation and curriculum summary
=========================================================

All runs use the same unchanged water4 validation video and select best.pt by
validation macro-F1.  Each was allowed to complete 100 epochs to reveal its
plateau; the selected checkpoint is not necessarily the final epoch.

Experiment                         Best epoch   Macro-F1   Accuracy   Level MAE
015_aggressive_rotation             37           0.5556     0.6667     0.6667
016_aggressive_affine               31           0.5556     0.6667     0.5000
017_affine_then_flip_patch          16           0.5556     0.6667     0.6667
018_all_augmentations_curriculum    24           0.5556     0.6667     0.6667

Decision
--------
Use experiment 016's best.pt as the current preferred model.  It equals the
highest macro-F1 and has the lowest ordinal level error.  Aggressive rotation
is an equally strong alternative by macro-F1.  Curriculum stages did not add
held-out improvement, therefore do not use their final checkpoints merely
because they saw more augmentations.

Segmentation gate
-----------------
With the supplied documented preprocessing (1/3/5/7/10/15/20-second channels,
frame0 correction, global normalisation and Otsu/morphology ROI), the frozen
U-Net has held-out water4 Dice 0.8597 and IoU 0.7539.  It can now replace the
label-derived mask in a next end-to-end classifier experiment.

Statistical limit
-----------------
Only one independent validation video is available, and it guided all choices.
These are screening results rather than final generalisation estimates.
