Experiment 058 results
======================

Status: completed 20 CUDA epochs.  Best checkpoint: epoch 9 (selected on water2).

Protocol
--------
Experiment 051 plus an additional water60-only temporal augmentation on 234 of
320 training samples: onset delay 0.20--0.60 s, heating time scale 0.78--0.90,
and post-peak cooling scale 0.70--0.85.  The range was measured from the
water4-water60 curve, and was applied to both ConvGRU and U-Net time inputs.

Results
-------
water2: macro IoU 0.435199, water60 IoU 0.318305.
water4: macro IoU 0.246991, water60 IoU 0.098765.

Compared with experiment 051 (water2/water4 macro IoU 0.696554/0.670653), this
class-specific transform recovers a small nonzero water60 response on water4
but severely degrades every other class and is not a viable model replacement.

Decision
--------
Do not ship this checkpoint.  The remaining water60 issue cannot be resolved
reliably from a single water1 training curve by wider or class-specific
synthetic temporal warps.  A full six-level MVP needs additional labelled
water60 recordings spanning the deployment heating/cooling profile.  Until
then, either hold the full-class MVP or explicitly expose water60 as an
uncertain/unsupported level rather than returning a confident wrong class.
