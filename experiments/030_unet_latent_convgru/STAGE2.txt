Stage 2: ConvGRU hyperparameter search
=======================================

Feature fusion is fixed to frozen U-Net v1 e4 only.  All trials use the same
train=water1+augmentations / validation=water2 split and the same 60-frame,
30-second full-panel input.  The already-completed 031 configuration is the
two-layer 160-channel candidate C.  This stage runs the remaining candidates:

  033: one layer, 128 channels, lr 3e-4, wd 1e-4
  034: one layer, 160 channels, lr 2e-4, wd 1e-4
  035: two layers, 192 channels, dropout .10, lr 1e-4, wd 5e-4

Each uses the 50-epoch cap, scheduler and early stopping defined in the plan.
