Experiment 039 result
=====================

Training completed automatically at epoch 61 after 30 epochs without a new
macro-IoU maximum.  The best checkpoint is epoch 31, not an early epoch.

Optimization dynamics
---------------------
Train loss: 2.2154 at epoch 1 -> 0.0171 at epoch 61.
Minimum validation loss: 1.9842 at epoch 2.
Validation loss at the macro-IoU checkpoint: 3.9066.
Validation loss at epoch 61: 4.7652.
Best validation macro-IoU: 0.1781 at epoch 31.

This proves strong overfitting: training loss continues down while validation
loss rises.  Hard macro-IoU remains noisy and peaks much later than validation
loss, so the two criteria do not select the same checkpoint.

Visual and confusion-matrix inspection
--------------------------------------
The model locates most rectangular defects, but learns a near-deterministic
wrong permutation for four water classes on water2:
  true water40  -> predicted water80 (93.4%)
  true water60  -> predicted water100 (92.4%)
  true water80  -> predicted water60 (95.2%)
  true water100 -> predicted water40 (99.6%)

It also predicts a large false defect on the reflection below the panel because
the documented panel ROI is not applied in this training pipeline.

Root cause and next experiment
------------------------------
The three existing patched videos translate all six cells as one rigid group,
so their relative class positions never change.  They do not prevent spatial
class memorization.  The next controlled augmentation must move each labelled
cell independently/permutationally while preserving its complete thermal curve
and mask label.

FP16 diagnostic
---------------
U-Net v1 e3/e4 activations are roughly an order of magnitude larger than v2.
FP16 produced NaN ConvGRU gradients and silently skipped every update.  BF16
produced finite gradients and was used for this result.
