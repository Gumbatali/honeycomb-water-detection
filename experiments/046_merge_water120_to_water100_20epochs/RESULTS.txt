Experiment 046 results
======================

Status: completed 20 epochs on CUDA in the studcamp environment.
Selected checkpoint: epoch 6 (artifacts/best.pt).

Label protocol
--------------
Original water120 label 6 is mapped to water100 label 5 before loss and metric
calculation.  The model has six outputs: background plus water20, water40,
water60, water80 and combined water100/water120.  Original masks on disk were
not modified.

Comparative water4 evaluation

check rather than a fresh unbiased final test.

class 1 IoU: 0.539235

class 5 IoU (water100/120): 0.515112

Under the same merged water4 target protocol, experiment 045 scores 0.459851
macro IoU and experiment 046 scores 0.318063.  The merged class occupies about
twice the area of the other defect classes and receives the same per-pixel CE
weight.  The new model predicts class 5 for 20.3% of valid water4 pixels and
does not predict classes 3 or 4.  Therefore unweighted merging improves water2
but does not generalize better to water4 and should not replace 045 yet.

Recommendation
--------------
Keep the semantically correct water120->water100 mapping, but test it in a new
controlled experiment with class-5 cross-entropy weight 0.5 to compensate for
the doubled supervised area.  Filling water120 with neutral background is not
recommended: it creates a physically false negative and is impossible at
deployment unless the water120 region is already known.  If water120 truly
must be excluded, ignoring its loss as in experiment 045 is safer than teaching
the model that it is background.
