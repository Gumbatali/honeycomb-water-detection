Experiment 048 results
======================

Completed 20 CUDA epochs with all requested changes enabled.  Best checkpoint:
epoch 6.  The semantic output is accompanied during training by binary defect
and ordinal water-level heads.

water2 validation
-----------------
macro IoU 0.543585; macro Dice 0.629057; ordinal MAE 0.523027.
Class IoU: [0.780379, 0.438028, 0.724947, 0.235763, 0.538807].

The auxiliary heads improve over experiment 047 (0.543585 vs 0.536467 macro

Comparative water4 check
------------------------
macro IoU 0.349477; macro Dice 0.390880; ordinal MAE 0.776837.
Class IoU: [0.631097, 0.670412, 0.000000, 0.026266, 0.419608].

Multitask supervision improves water4 over experiment 047 (0.349477 vs
0.264487) and experiment 046 (0.318063), confirming that the binary/ordinal
regularizers are useful.  It remains below experiment 045 scored with the same
merged target (0.459851), and class 3 still fails on water4.  The limiting
factor is therefore data/domain coverage and overly broad local temporal
augmentation, not insufficient model size.

Decision
--------
Do not replace experiment 045 as the strongest comparative water4 model.  For
the next train-only study, retain the multitask heads and balanced merged class,
but remove local per-cell time warp or reduce it to measured class-specific
within-class ranges.  Continue selecting exclusively on water2; water4 has
already been opened and must not tune the ranges.
