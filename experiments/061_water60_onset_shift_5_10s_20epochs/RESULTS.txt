Experiment 061 results
======================

Status: completed 20 CUDA epochs.  Best checkpoint: epoch 19.

Protocol
--------
Experiment 051 plus water60-only onset delay sampled uniformly from 5 to 10
seconds on 218 of 320 training samples (mean 7.60 seconds).  Heating and
cooling scales were unchanged; all other experiment-051 settings were retained.

Results
-------
water2 validation: macro IoU 0.599019, water60 IoU 0.465501.
water4 comparative check: macro IoU 0.464972, water60 IoU 0.000000.

Decision
--------
Do not replace experiment 051.  The 5--10 s delay retains more validation
performance than the 0--30 s stress test, but lowers water4 macro IoU from
0.670653 to 0.464972 and does not recover water60.  Together with experiments
058 and 060, this rules out onset-delay augmentation alone as a useful remedy.
