Experiment 061: water60-only onset shift 5--10 seconds
========================================================

Controlled extension of experiment 051.  On 70% of training draws, only
water60 is delayed uniformly by 5--10 seconds.  No heating or cooling time
scale is applied.  All other domain and global temporal augmentation remains
unchanged, and the transform is applied consistently to ConvGRU and U-Net time
inputs.
