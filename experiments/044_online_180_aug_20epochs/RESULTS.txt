Train/validation convergence
----------------------------
At epochs 2, 3 and 8, absolute |validation loss - train loss| was respectively
0.0586, 0.0449 and 0.0109.  Validation therefore closely follows the augmented
training distribution during the first eight epochs.  The best semantic
checkpoint occurs at epoch 6.

After epoch 9 the losses diverge.  At epoch 20 train loss is 0.4369 while
validation loss is 1.8066 and macro-IoU falls to 0.3326.  Augmentation closes
the initial domain gap and delays overfitting, but does not justify training
for all 20 epochs.  For this setup the useful range is approximately 5--8
epochs, selected by validation macro-IoU.

The observed frequencies agree with the requested probabilities.  Validation
