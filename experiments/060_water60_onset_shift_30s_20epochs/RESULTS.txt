Experiment 060 results
======================

Status: completed 20 CUDA epochs.  Best checkpoint: epoch 11.

Protocol
--------
Experiment 051 plus water60-only positive onset delay sampled uniformly from
0 to 30 seconds on 218 of 320 training samples.  Heating and cooling scales
are unchanged.  The mean active delay was 15.62 seconds; large delays can move
the water60 response outside the 0--29.5 second model input.

Results
-------
water2 validation: macro IoU 0.638009, water60 IoU 0.439797.
water4 comparative check: macro IoU 0.513544, water60 IoU 0.000000.

Decision
--------
Do not replace experiment 051.  A wide onset-shift distribution preserves more
of water2 than experiment 058, but it still lowers water4 macro IoU from
0.670653 to 0.513544 and does not recover water60.  Therefore delayed onset
alone is not the water60 domain failure mechanism.
