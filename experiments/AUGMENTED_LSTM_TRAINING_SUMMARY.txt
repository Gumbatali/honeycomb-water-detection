40-epoch CUDA LSTM augmentation training summary
=================================================

Protocol
--------
For each experiment, training contains original water1/water2 and only the
corresponding augmentation of those videos: 24 labelled cell sequences total.
Validation is always the unaugmented water4 video (6 sequences).  This avoids
cross-video leakage.  Model: mask-guided CNN + bidirectional LSTM (hidden 64)
with ordinal six-level water head.  Each run completed 40 epochs; best.pt is
chosen by validation macro-F1.

Experiment                 Best epoch   Macro-F1   Accuracy   Level MAE

011_horizontal_flip        21           0.3889     0.5000     1.0000
012_geometric_affine       18           0.3333     0.5000     1.1667
013_background_patching     9           0.3611     0.5000     0.6667
014_defect_location_shift  21           0.4444     0.5000     0.8333

Selection
---------
The location-shift augmentation (014) has the highest macro-F1 and is the
current candidate checkpoint: experiments/014_defect_location_shift/artifacts/best.pt.
Patching produces the lowest ordinal MAE but lower macro-F1; it is useful as a
robustness augmentation, not the selected standalone classifier.

Limit
-----
Only three independent videos exist.  water4 guided both model selection and
the augmentation comparison, so these are validation-screening values, not a
final generalisation estimate.  Evaluate the chosen checkpoint on a new video
and use frozen predicted, rather than label-derived, segmentation masks.
