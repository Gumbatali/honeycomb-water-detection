Experiment 062 results
======================

Status: completed 20 CUDA epochs.  Best checkpoint: epoch 16.

Label protocol
--------------
There is exactly one merged pair: original water60 (3) and water80 (4) become
one compact output class 3.  Original water100 (5) becomes compact output class
4.  Original water120 (6) is neutralized in the inputs and ignored in target,
loss and metrics; it is not merged with another label.  The combined class has
CE weight 0.5 to compensate for its doubled supervised area.

Augmentation
------------
23 training records per epoch: 16 online draws from water1, three translated
defect-patch videos, and four neutral-canvas gain-permutation videos.  Every
draw also uses the experiment-051 online domain and global temporal transforms.
Observed over 460 draws: 224 180-degree rotations, 336 scale/translations, 238
cell permutations, 322 local gains, 229 onset shifts, 228 time warps, and 134
cooling warps.  Flip, aggressive affine and unconstrained random pasting were
excluded because they had degraded robustness in prior tests.

Metrics
-------
water2 validation macro IoU: 0.783578
  water20 0.942779; water40 0.530667; water60/water80 0.761955; water100 0.898913

water4 comparative macro IoU: 0.760867
  water20 0.565957; water40 0.918396; water60/water80 0.620758; water100 0.938356

Fair comparison with experiment 051
-----------------------------------
If 051's saved water4 confusion matrix is remapped to the same deployed four
defect classes, its macro IoU is 0.764212 versus 0.760867 here.  Thus 062 does
not improve aggregate performance, but it raises the deployed water60/water80
IoU from 0.562419 to 0.620758 and removes the unsupported standalone water60
decision.  The cost is primarily water20 IoU (0.658156 to 0.565957).

Decision
--------
062 is the appropriate MVP candidate only if the product may expose a combined
"water60--water80" level rather than distinct water60 and water80 levels.
For a six-level product, retain 051 and label water60 as unsupported/uncertain;
the present data does not validate a separate water60 classifier.
