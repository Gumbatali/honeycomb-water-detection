Experiment 042 result
=====================

Neutral-canvas independent cell permutations with local defect-contrast gains
from 0.90 to 1.10.  Fresh U-Net v1 pyramid ConvGRU with ROI and pixel-peak
temporal normalization.  Early stopped at epoch 80; best epoch 50.

Best water2 metrics:
  macro-IoU:   0.4092
  macro-Dice:  0.4545
  ordinal MAE: 0.8194

Per-class IoU:
  water20:  0.9458
  water40:  0.8123
  water60:  0.2877
  water80:  0.0000
  water100: 0.0000

Comparison
----------
041, no local gain: macro-IoU 0.4078, best epoch 29.
042, +/-10% gain:   macro-IoU 0.4092, best epoch 50.

Local gain gives a small +0.0013 absolute macro-IoU improvement and slightly
better water60 IoU (0.2354 -> 0.2877), while water40 drops modestly.  Its main
benefit is radiometric robustness rather than a large validation gain.  The
measured water2 5-second response is 7.9% higher than water1, so the chosen gain
range is physically relevant.

The validation image shows accurate ROI-constrained segmentation and correct
water20/water40 labels.  Both high-water cells are mapped mostly to water60,
indicating that categorical CE+Dice still does not exploit ordinal water-level
structure.  The next architecture/loss change should add an ordinal term or a
separate per-cell temporal regressor while keeping the full-frame segmentation
branch.

neutral_water1 remains unchanged and available as the canvas for additional
patching variants.

training_dynamics.png, water2_validation.png, water2_confusion_matrix.json.
