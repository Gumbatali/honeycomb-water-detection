TensorBoard for LSTM and augmentation experiments
==================================================

Start locally:
  ./experiments/run_tensorboard.sh

Open http://127.0.0.1:6006 .

The exporter writes events to experiments/tensorboard_logs/ and includes:
  - epoch-level validation loss, accuracy, macro-F1 and ordinal MAE for
    experiments 001--003;
  - the best-run summary for each LSTM experiment;
  - three QA diagrams per augmentation experiment 010--014, each showing
    source frame, transformed frame, source labels and transformed labels.

Rerunning run_tensorboard.sh regenerates the event logs from the saved metrics
and diagrams, so no stale dashboard state is retained.
