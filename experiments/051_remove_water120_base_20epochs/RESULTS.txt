Experiment 051 results
======================

Status: completed all 20 epochs on CUDA.  Best checkpoint: epoch 18.

Removal protocol
----------------
water120 is absent from model supervision and evaluation.  Its thermal pixels
in both the 60-frame GRU input and seven-frame U-Net input are replaced by the
nearest normal-panel response from a local 31x31 neighborhood.  Its target
pixels are ignore=255.  Original files remain unchanged.

Compared with experiment 045, neutralizing rather than merely ignoring the
water120 input raises water2 macro IoU from 0.593125 to 0.696554 (+17.4%
relative), lowers validation loss from 0.770424 to 0.624068 and improves
ordinal MAE from 0.302673 to 0.229036.

Comparative water4 evaluation

Relative to experiment 045, water4 macro IoU rises from 0.547147 to 0.670653
(+22.6% relative).  water80 and water100 improve especially strongly.  The
water60 failure remains unchanged: the model does not emit class 3 on water4.
This is now isolated as a class-specific recording/domain issue rather than a
water120 side effect.

Decision
--------
Experiment 051 is the strongest model in the current series on both water2 and
comparative water4 metrics.  Use artifacts/best.pt, never the final epoch.
The next work should target water60 with new real recordings or a separately
measured class-specific transform; do not reintroduce class-agnostic cell warp.
