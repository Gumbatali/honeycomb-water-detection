Adding absolute temperature beside the independently normalized contrast does

channel scales from one training recording, and the contrast channel discards
amplitude information.  Experiment 051 remains decisively better: 0.696554

Decision
--------
Do not select 053 or 054.  The local-background idea is not rejected, because
053 exposes some water60 signal.  The rejected component is per-pixel contrast
normalization.  A future controlled variant should scale the entire contrast
video by one panel-level robust percentile (or by the local-background peak),
preserving relative amplitude between cells, and should project each input
channel separately before fusion.
