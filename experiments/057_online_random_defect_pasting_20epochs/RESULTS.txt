Experiment 057 results
======================

Status: completed 20 CUDA epochs.  Best checkpoint: epoch 10 (selected only on water2).

Training augmentation
---------------------
Experiment 051 plus online random defect pasting.  On 142 of 320 training
draws (44.4%), one to three water20..water100 residual patches were pasted into
independently sampled non-overlapping positions inside the neutral water1 panel.
This yielded 273 pasted patches total.  water120 was not pasted; random pasting
was mutually exclusive with cell permutation.

water2 validation
-----------------
macro IoU:   0.430385
macro Dice:  0.486460
ordinal MAE: 0.530660

water4 comparative check
------------------------
macro IoU:   0.260756
macro Dice:  0.277676
ordinal MAE: 1.117018

Decision
--------
Do not replace experiment 051.  Relative to 051, macro IoU drops from 0.696554
to 0.430385 on water2 and from 0.670653 to 0.260756 on water4.  Pasting only
one to three isolated cells onto a neutral canvas disrupts the real panel/grid
context and produces an overly sparse training target.  A follow-up should use
realistically constrained whole-grid placements or a small mixture weight, not
this unconstrained patch-pasting distribution.
