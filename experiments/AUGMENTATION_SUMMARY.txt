Label-aware video augmentation summary
======================================

Created virtual videos: 15
  3 source videos x 5 augmentation experiments

Experiment                  Transform                         Label handling
010_rotation                +6 degree rotation                nearest-neighbour mask + transformed bbox
011_horizontal_flip         left-right reflection              reflected mask + reflected bbox
012_geometric_affine        -4 degree, 0.98 scale, shear       affine mask + affine bbox
013_background_patching     four 12x12 background bias patches mask and bbox unchanged by design
014_defect_location_shift   +8 px x, -6 px y                  translated mask + translated bbox

All geometric parameters are fixed per generated video and do not vary from
frame to frame.  Thus the thermal curve of every material point is preserved;
only its spatial location/view changes.  Class IDs are never interpolated:
semantic masks are warped with nearest-neighbour interpolation while thermal
frames are warped linearly.

Locations
---------
  manifests: data/synthetic/video_augmentation_manifests/<experiment>/
  QA plots:  experiments/<experiment>/plots/

The manifests contain source_video, transform parameters, transformed cells
with bbox_xyxy, and, for patching, deterministic patch coordinates.  They are
virtual videos to avoid storing approximately 27 GB of duplicated 3000-frame
arrays.  To materialize all image/mask frames deliberately:

  /home/votter/miniconda3/envs/studcamp/bin/python \
    scripts/create_video_augmentation_experiments.py --materialize

This writes transformed images and semantic masks together under
data/honeycomb/synthetic/materialized/.  Do not materialize a train/validation source
onto the other side of a video-level split: an augmentation of water1 or
water2 belongs only to training, and an augmentation of water4 only to
validation/test.
