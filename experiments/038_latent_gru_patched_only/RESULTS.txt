Experiment 038 result
=====================

Purpose
-------
Test whether translated defect patches help without adding the artificial
all-background neutral video that degraded experiment 037.

Configuration
-------------
Architecture: frozen U-Net v1 e4 latent features + full-frame thermal sequence
+ one 128-channel ConvGRU layer.  Input is 60 equally spaced frames covering
0--29.5 seconds.  Training contains water1, the seven existing augmentations,
and three translated defect-patch videos.  water120 is ignored.  Validation is
the unchanged water2 video.  Warm start: experiment 037 best.pt; learning rate
3e-5; AdamW weight decay 1e-4; maximum 30 epochs; early stopping patience 15.

Result
------
Training stopped automatically at epoch 17.  Best checkpoint is epoch 2.
Best five-class validation macro-IoU: 0.2242.

Per-class validation IoU:
  water20:  0.7030
  water40:  0.1753
  water60:  0.2429
  water80:  0.0000
  water100: 0.0000

Comparison on the same five classes:
  experiment 033 baseline:             0.1863
  experiment 037 neutral + patches:    0.1783
  experiment 038 patches without neutral: 0.2242

Conclusion
----------
Patched-only fine-tuning improves macro-IoU by 0.0380 absolute (20.4% relative)
over the comparable baseline.  The neutral all-background video should remain
excluded.  The checkpoint still fails to identify water80 and water100 on
water2, so 038 is the current best tuning checkpoint but not a final model.
water4 remains untouched for a single final unbiased evaluation.
